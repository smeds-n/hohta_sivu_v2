Is Hohta right for me?

Hohta is a strong fit for companies. Hohta's monitoring device does not need to connect to your internal company network, which avoids a long IT approval process. Instead, the monitoring device has its own mobile internet connection. We can also tailor the information display to match your brand identity. Logos, colors, displayed metrics, or a fully custom interface are all available. Learn more about Hohta's features here or book a demo.
