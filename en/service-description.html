Hohta Basic

Hohta hardware: €600 excluding VAT, one-time. Basic display with no monthly fee.

Schools, non-profits and other educational institutions can contact us for a quote. An optional cellular plan is €6/month including VAT; it keeps the device outside your network for added security.

Show current production power and total yield from your solar installation on your existing information screen.

The package includes

  • a Hohta connection device for collecting data from one compatible inverter
  • a public solar-production display accessible in a browser
  • current production power and total yield
  • Hohta's standard design

The package's compatibility limit is one inverter. There is no maximum rated installation capacity. We check the inverter make, model and required data connection before you order.

You can use your own installer and your site's internet connection or your own mobile data plan. Installation, any preconfiguration or commissioning assistance and delivery charges are agreed separately before ordering.

Your own installer can carry out physical installation. Configuration, commissioning assistance and later changes can be ordered from Hohta separately. We agree the work and price before starting.

Detailed delivery information

The standard hardware consists of a Teltonika RUT206 router, power supply, two mobile antennas, a Wi-Fi antenna, DIN mounting kit and a ten-year Teltonika RMS Management licence for one device. Procurement and packaging are included in the one-time €600 price, excluding VAT. Site-specific additional hardware and delivery charges are stated before ordering. The RMS licence covers the manufacturer's remote management service; Hohta's technical work is separately agreed.

The basic display is hosted on Hohta's own server equipment in Finland. It is intended for presenting solar production. No uptime percentage or response and repair times are agreed as part of delivery. Premium's monitoring view and error reporting are implemented as agreed; any service level is separately agreed.

You receive the terms and delivery scope for acceptance before ordering.
